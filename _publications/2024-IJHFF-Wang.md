A non-dimensional analysis of the electric field-enhanced nucleate boiling process has been conducted and a numerical solver developed on the OpenFOAM-v2012 framework has been utilized. The dynamic characteristics of bubble deformation and detachment process in electric field-enhanced nucleate boiling process has been demonstrated and analyzed in detail. The effects of gravity field and electric field on the heat transfer performance of nuclear boiling process is quantitatively compared to reveal its internal heat transfer mechanism. The heat transfer capacity of the system has been quantified. Both the two-dimensional and three-dimensional numerical simulations has been conducted, and the difference in results are compared, which is conducive to a more adequate evaluation of the numerical results.
